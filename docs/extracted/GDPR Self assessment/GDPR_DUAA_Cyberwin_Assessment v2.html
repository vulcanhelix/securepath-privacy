Section 01
UK GDPR Art. 5/24DPA 2018
Governance & Accountability

Accountability is a core UK GDPR principle (Art. 5(2)). Organisations must demonstrate compliance through documented frameworks, designated roles, maintained records and staff training.

Section 02
UK GDPR Art. 6/7/12-22DUAA 2025
Lawful Basis & Data Subject Rights

Every processing activity requires a lawful basis under Art. 6. The DUAA 2025 added Recognised Legitimate Interests and a new statutory right to complain directly to controllers.

Section 03
UK GDPR Art. 25/32/33-35DUAA 2025
Data Management & Security

Privacy by Design (Art. 25), proportionate security measures (Art. 32), 72-hour breach notification (Art. 33), DPIAs (Art. 35), and the DUAA 2025 updated international transfer test.
