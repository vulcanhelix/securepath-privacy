# Reporting a problem — quick guide

You don't need any technical knowledge. The whole job is: **tell us what you saw, in your own words.** This takes about a minute each time.

🎬 **Watch first:** `uat-walkthrough.mp4` (in this folder) — a 2-minute video showing the whole thing below, end to end.

## Step 1 — Accept your invite

You'll get an email inviting you to **Linear** (that's where reports live). Click the link, choose a password, and you're in. You only do this once.

## Step 2 — File a report

1. In Linear, click **New Issue** (top of the screen, or press the `C` key).
2. Where it asks for a template, choose **UAT report**.
3. The form asks four things — answer them in your own words:

   - **What happened?** — what you saw on screen
   - **What did you expect?** — what you thought should happen
   - **Which screen were you on?** — the page name, or just describe it
   - **Screenshot** — attach one if you can (it helps a lot, but it's fine without)

4. Click **Create issue**. Done — your report is in.

## Step 3 — What happens next (you do nothing here)

Your report is checked and worked on automatically. One of two things comes back as a comment on your report:

- **A short video** showing the problem fixed — watch it.
- **A friendly explanation** if what you saw turns out to be correct behaviour — with a plain-language reason, not jargon.

## Step 4 — Reply

- If the video looks right: reply **"yes, that's fixed"** (any wording is fine) and it ships for everyone.
- If it's still wrong or confusing: reply and say so — it goes back for another look.

## Good to know

- **One problem per report** is easiest, but don't stress — file what you see.
- **There is no wrong report.** A report that turns out to be fine is always better than staying quiet.
- You never need to reproduce it twice or answer "can you try that again" — a screenshot the first time is enough.
- If we can't reproduce it, we'll ask on the ticket — that's the only time you'll hear "can you show me".
